Getting started

Open an account with Alpine

Becoming an Alpine client gives you access to our institutional broking desk, corporate deal flow and small & mid-cap ECM transactions. Here's how it works.

  1. 1

    Get in touch

    Reach out to the Alpine team to register your interest. We'll arrange an introductory call to understand your objectives, time horizon and the kind of opportunities you're looking for.

    Email the team →
  2. 2

    Confirm your investor classification

    We confirm whether you qualify as a wholesale, sophisticated or professional investor under s708 of the Corporations Act. Where required, we will request an accountant's certificate.

  3. 3

    Complete your application & verification

    You'll complete a short application and identity verification (AML/KYC). Our team handles the paperwork with you and answers any questions along the way.

  4. 4

    Define the service that's right for you

    Together, we'll define the service that's right for you. From full portfolio construction and wealth management to trading and placement services, we'll tailor our approach to your objectives and risk appetite.

  5. 5

    Activate your trading account

    Your account is set up on our execution platform (via FinClear / Morrisons), giving you personalised execution, trade reporting and access to designated trading representatives.

  6. 6

    Start investing

    You're live. Access Alpine's corporate deal flow, small & mid-cap ECM placements, pre-IPO and unlisted opportunities, with ongoing aftermarket support from our team.
